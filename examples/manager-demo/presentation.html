Seven lines of business policy

version: 1
connection: connection.yaml
workloads:
  payments:
    topic: payments/{account}/{event}
    keep_together: account
    subscribers: [ledger, audit]

Automatic scaling, up to four brokers and one warm reserve are defaults. The operator profile holds endpoints, credentials references and measured capacity.

The important proof

Kill the Go publisher with 24 accepted payments still on disk. Restart it during migration. Reconcile every ID in ledger and audit, with account order preserved.

Guarantee: at-least-once delivery. Business handlers commit deduplication with the payment update. A broker ACK is a background transport receipt, not a subscriber API response.
